## 2026-10-13 - First version: all the Gemini helpers in one folder
- Made by: Claude (from Gemini's files)
- Changed: Master **AI Mode** switch (Settings, Privacy; on by default; off means nothing is sent). Assignment tools: **Make it clear** (A60), **Message my professor** with More formal and Friendlier (A61, A92), **Ask about this assignment** (A84), and a **pre-submission check**. In Help me start: **break a stuck step into 3 pieces** and **3 warm-up practice questions**. On Assignments: **Sort my brain dump** (A26, A94). On Today: **Planning helpers** (tune today's plan, split time across classes, break a big project into milestones). In Settings, Display: **Describe how you work** and a **custom color palette**. On the major page: **Personalize for my major** (A38) with **saved routines** (A93, shown in Help me start). Each has a built-in version for when AI Mode is off or Gemini cannot be reached.
- Why: Christian asked for these Gemini features to be added in their own subfolder.
- Notes: Every result is shown for review and nothing is saved until you tap Apply or Add (A4). Grades, scores and personal notes are never sent, and the server only keeps the fields it names (unit tests check this). **Not in the decision log yet:** planning helpers, website and palette personalizer, step breaker, practice questions, pre-flight check and the AI Mode switch (the rest are A26, A38, A60, A61, A84, A92, A93, A94). **Changes from the pasted code:** the palette is checked on all 8 contrast pairs and cannot be used if any fail (the original checked 2 and silently swapped colors); day-plan changes cap at 6 tasks and keep the plan unconfirmed; milestone dates must fall between today and the due date and the original task can be moved to Not now so work is not counted twice; brain-dump tasks get a chosen course and due date instead of "due today"; the built-in keyword matchers only change what the text mentions; all pasted schema defaults that would have turned celebrations on or reset saved-data version were left out. **Not tested with a real Gemini key.**
