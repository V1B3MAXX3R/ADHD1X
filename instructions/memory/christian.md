Christian owns ADHD1X (a personal school-helper website) and approves every decision. He is a part-time coder: he can spot basic problems but does not write much code, so he relies on AI helpers and on tests. He works on Windows 11 in VS Code, with the project inside a OneDrive folder.

**Why this matters:** he needs plain, honest explanations and small steps, not jargon or long surveys.

**How to apply:**
- Explain what you did and what he needs to decide, in plain words. Lead with the result.
- When there is a real choice, give a recommendation and ask once. Do not list every option.
- He often gives broad goals ("clean up the project", "make a clock"). Make sensible choices, say which you made, and check them against the decision log.
- Treat his product decisions as final once he has made them. Do not re-argue them.
