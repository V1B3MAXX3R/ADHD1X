metadata:

The finish pop-up (voyage reward and fun fact) must stay a switch each person can turn on or off, off by default. Christian confirmed this twice: "it is an option for someone to change" and "no keep it as an option to toggle".

**Why:** decision A5 says gamification is optional, and A24 and A67 say fun facts can be turned off.

**How to apply:** it shows only in Space style AND when Settings, Display, "Small celebration when I finish an assignment" is on. Do not make it on by default, and do not add other rewards that cannot be switched off.
