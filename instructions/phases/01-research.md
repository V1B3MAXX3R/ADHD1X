# Phase 1: Scientific Research and User Needs

**Version 1.1.1** (2026-10-10)

**Status:** Complete. Christian approved moving to Phase 2 on 2026-10-10 (A95).
**Current report:** [ADHD1X Phase 1 Research Report](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/be0199da-9277-4620-92b6-cfc034293509)

## Goal
Understand ADHD, Autism, and AuDHD in university students well enough to know which needs are real, which product ideas have evidence, and which are just popular claims.

## Topics
1. History of ADHD and Autism diagnoses
2. Neurodiversity and affirming approaches
3. Underdiagnosis, masking, recognition gaps
4. Executive dysfunction, task initiation, working memory
5. Time perception and duration estimates
6. Sensory overload, routines, transitions
7. Co-occurring ADHD and Autism
8. Burnout, fatigue, energy
9. Accommodation and productivity strategies that work
10. First-person accounts and complaints about existing tools

Evidence checks required: body doubling, dopamine rewards, task chunking, Zeigarnik effect, interest-based motivation, sensory-load tracking.

## Each finding must include
Finding, source, strength and limits of evidence, user difficulty, possible product implication.

## Inputs
Master prompt sections 1, 2, 3 (Phase 1), 4 (feature claims to test).

## Deliverable
Structured research report: user needs, research gaps, accessibility considerations, product opportunities, and the decisions to make before ideation.

## Gate
Stop after the report. Christian approves before Phase 2 starts.
